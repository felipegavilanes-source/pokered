# Pokémon Blue — Starter & Difficulty Edition

Development branch: `challenge-edition`. Original Pokémon Blue characters, trainers,
cities, story, and visuals are retained.

## Starter choices
- Professor Oak offers **Magikarp**, **Tangela**, or **Zubat**, each at level 5.
- Rival teams use their corresponding evolutions later in the game.
- **Bulbasaur** appears as a rare wild encounter on Route 24.
- **Charmander** appears as a rare wild encounter on Route 25.
- **Squirtle** appears as a rare wild encounter on Route 6.
- Magikarp starts with **Tackle** so it can participate in battles.

## Difficulty
- Trainer levels are generally increased by 5–10 levels.
- Major opponents have stronger, larger teams.
- Brock has six Pokémon, levels 20–22.
- Improved battle AI and move selection for bosses and rivals.
- The rival can use healing items moderately.

## Removed customizations
Famous-person trainers, Agumon, custom trainer portraits/sprites, the extra
Champion Island event, bonus starting items, altered town names, and unrelated
changes to wild encounters and trade evolutions have been removed.

## Build
Build with `make blue`, run `python3 tools/check_challenge_core.py`,
or download `pokeblue.gbc` from a successful branch build artifact.
